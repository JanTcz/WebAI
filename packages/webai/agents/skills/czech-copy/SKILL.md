# Czech text on the web

- Quotes „like this“ (low 99, high 66), nested ‚like this‘.
- Non-breaking space (`&nbsp;` in HTML/Astro, U+00A0 in data) after the one-letter
  words **k s v z o u a i** and between a number and its unit (`10&nbsp;%`, `5&nbsp;kB`).
- Dash with spaces – like this (en dash); ranges without spaces: 9–17 h.
- Thousands separated by a non-breaking space: 12&nbsp;500 Kč.
- `s.r.o.` always with periods, likewise `a.s.`.
- Buttons and links: a verb in the imperative or a clear target („Napište nám“,
  „Ceník služeb“), not „Klikněte zde“ ("click here").
- Keep `meta description` between 120 and 160 characters. No period at the end of headings.
- Keep the tone of the surrounding text (formal "vy" address, sentence length). If the
  project has content tests (lengths, card counts), respect them.
